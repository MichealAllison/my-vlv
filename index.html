About Us

What is VLV?

VivaLaVida is a connected business group that brings together three specialist companies. Ambidexters provides technology and digital solutions. SkillHubs builds practical capability through training and consultancy. ImpactCity supports visibility, positioning and growth.

Each company can support you independently or connect with the others when your business needs cross more than one area.
